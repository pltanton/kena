- αιτιατική: αντικείμενο

| αιτ.   | πόσους | πόσες | πόσα  |
  • **bold**, *italic*, `code`, ~~struck~~, [link](https://…).
  • Lines starting with - or 1.: a list. An empty line starts a new paragraph.
  • A table: a header row, a |---| row, then one row per line. The columns don't have to line up.
  • No HTML and no images; they are not shown.
